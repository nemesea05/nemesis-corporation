The Nemesis community

A shared space around Nemesis Corporation and its four divisions, for partners, collaborators, and curious people who want to follow what we build.

About the community

Where our work meets the people it is for.

Nemesis Corporation works through partnerships, creative work, and technology. The community is for the people who take part in that work, across all four divisions, and keeps them informed about what the company is doing.

What we stand for

  • Sustainable growth

    Growth that lasts, built on a solid foundation and a long-term view.

  • Strong partnerships

    Partnerships that benefit everyone involved, and support for early-stage businesses.

  • Steady improvement

    Adapting to changing markets and improving our solutions step by step.

How we treat each other

Simple expectations for everyone who takes part in the community.

  • Be respectfulDisagree with ideas, never with people. Harassment and hate have no place here.
  • Protect privacyDo not share personal details about yourself or anyone else without permission.
  • Keep it honestShare what you know to be true, and say so when you are not sure.
  • Stay on purposeSpam, scams, and unrelated promotion are not welcome.
