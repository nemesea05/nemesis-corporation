NEM Holdings

Finance and investments for the group

NEM Holdings handles the group's finances. It manages company assets and investments, and makes sure each division has the resources it needs to operate.

Core focus

Managing corporate assets, investments, and resource allocation.

Key objective

Keep the group financially stable and make careful investments that support the other divisions.

Initiatives

Three areas of work that carry the mandate of NEM Holdings.

  1. Asset management

    Looks after the group's assets and keeps track of its financial health.

  2. Investments

    Makes careful investments that support the group's long-term plans and the other divisions.

  3. Resource allocation

    Shares capital and resources across Nemesis Corporation so each division has what it needs to operate.
