Four divisions. One company.

Nemesis Corporation is a startup company based in Quezon City. We work through four divisions: Holdings, Ventures, Media, and Technologies.

Executive summary

A startup company with four divisions that look for business opportunities, support new ideas, and build solutions that can grow over time.

Our vision is to grow steadily and responsibly, with a strong foundation and creative ideas.

Corporate divisions

Each division has its own role. Together they make up Nemesis Corporation.

Point to a division to see its details.

  1. NEM Holdings

    Finance and investments

    NEM Holdings handles the group's finances. It manages company assets and investments, and makes sure each division has the resources it needs to operate.

    Core focus
    Managing corporate assets, investments, and resource allocation.
    Key objective
    Keep the group financially stable and make careful investments that support the other divisions.

  2. NEM Ventures

    Partnerships and new businesses

    NEM Ventures builds new businesses and works with partners. Its focus is partnerships, starting new ventures, and creating projects together with other companies.

    Core focus
    Strategic partnerships, venture building, and co-creation.
    Key objective
    Build lasting partnerships, support early-stage businesses, and develop new business ideas together with partners.

  3. NEM Media

    Communications and content

    NEM Media handles communications and creative work for the group. It produces content, runs digital marketing, and manages how the company presents itself to the public.

    Core focus
    Creative communications, digital marketing, content creation, and public relations.
    Key objective
    Keep the group's brand clear and consistent, connect with audiences, and share accurate information about Nemesis Corporation and its partners.

  4. NEM Technologies

    Software and digital tools

    NEM Technologies builds the group's software and digital tools. It develops software, works with data, and improves how the group uses technology.

    Core focus
    Technological innovation, software development, data solutions, and digital transformation.
    Key objective
    Build practical software and data solutions, simplify the group's operations, and improve how it uses technology.

Leadership

John Lloyd P. Lacumba

Founder and CEO

As Founder and CEO, John Lloyd P. Lacumba sets the direction for Nemesis Corporation and its four divisions: Holdings, Ventures, Media, and Technologies. The focus is sustainable growth, strong partnerships, and building a company that lasts.

Steady progress. Sustainable growth.

As a startup company, Nemesis Corporation plans to grow step by step, adapt to changing markets, and keep its four divisions working closely together.

Our focus is on creating lasting value for our partners and customers through reliable work.
